## Reading of the profile

### `/recrutare-it/` is doing all the work

Five of the top rankings come from one page: `/recrutare-it/`. It holds #1,
#1, #2, #4, and #21. This is a well-targeted service page, and it is the
reason Labs' homepage-only picture is misleading.

### Systematic homepage cannibalization

On four keywords, both `/recrutare-it/` **and** `/` rank — and the homepage
always lands far behind:

| Keyword | Service page | Homepage |
|---|---:|---:|
| agentie de recrutare it | 1 | 40 |
| firme de recrutare it | 1 | 49 |
| recrutare it romania | 4 | 57 |
| recrutare it bucuresti | 21 | 57 |

Both URLs target the same intent. The homepage is not adding reach; it is
splitting signals. This is also exactly what Labs captured — Labs saw only
the weak homepage rankings and missed every strong service-page one.

### A second domain competing with the first

`humanriseagency.com` — an English-language sister site — ranks on the same
Romanian queries:

| Keyword | humanrise.ro | humanriseagency.com |
|---|---:|---:|
| reverse recruitment | **1** | 4 |
| employer branding audit | 14 | **8** |

On employer branding the `.com` **outranks** the `.ro`. Two owned domains
competing for one query. `humanriseagency.com` has no Labs record either.

### English is unclaimed

`it recruitment romania` returns the homepage at #61 and the service page at
#75 — both effectively invisible. The site is `lang="ro-RO"` throughout with
no English tree; the `.com` sister site is not covering the gap on Romanian
SERPs either.

| recrutare personal | — | 140 |

The two sources disagree because they are different datasets: Labs carries
its own keyword_info volumes, Ads reports its own bucketing and suppresses
low-volume long-tail. Treat both as directional. The honest summary is that
these are low-volume, high-intent commercial terms — a #1 on `agentie de
recrutare it` is worth having, but it is not a traffic firehose.
